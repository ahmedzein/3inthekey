The Podcast

Introducing a fresh perspective on basketball through the eyes of passionate Canadian fans. This show goes beyond borders, delivering comprehensive coverage of everything basketball, from the NBA to NCAA, CEBL, USports, and the vibrant world of International Ball. Expect more than just updates – we dive deep into the game, offering in-depth analysis, insightful breakdowns, and captivating discussions. Tune in on demand using any Podcast app at your convenience and stay immersed in the exhilarating world of basketball with us.

Elias

A proud Ottawa native with a lifelong passion for basketball, especially the Lakers, Elias has built a career in the sport as a play-by-play announcer and sideline reporter. Formerly the voice of Carleton University Ravens basketball, he now reports for the Ottawa Blackjacks and hosts 3 In The Key, sharing his love for the game through dynamic discussions.

Fuad

Fuad channels the legacy of the GOAT and the iconic 90’s Chicago Bulls, with basketball becoming his lifelong passion thanks to their greatness and the magic of Space Jam. As our social media whiz, he keeps our pages buzzing—though as a die-hard Bulls fan, expect a little extra Bulls love along the way!
